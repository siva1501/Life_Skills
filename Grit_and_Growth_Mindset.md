# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit

Grit is the ability to work hard, face difficult moments in life, and keep moving forward with courage. It means having a strong belief in what we can achieve, having passion for our goals, and working consistently towards success.

## 2. Growth Mindset

As an aspiring software engineer, we should have a growth mindset. We should learn something new every day and keep moving towards our dreams.

We need to be dynamic and understand that there are no limitations to learning. We should always be ready to improve our knowledge and skills.

## 3. What is Internal Locus of Control? What is the Key Point in the Video?

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions, decisions, and efforts influence the outcomes of our lives.

I personally follow a quote from the Bhagavad Gita:

**"Karmanye Vadhikaraste Ma Phaleshu Kadachana."**

It means we have control over our actions, but not over the results. We should work hard for what we want without worrying too much about the outcome.

## 4. Key Points Mentioned by the Speaker to Build a Growth Mindset

1. Believe in your ability to figure things out.
2. Question your assumptions.
3. Develop your own life curriculum.
4. Honour the struggle.
